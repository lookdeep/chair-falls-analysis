# STROBE Checklist (Cohort Study) - Submission Supplement

Source guideline: STROBE statement for cohort studies.

## Items Covered in Manuscript

1. Title/Abstract: observational cohort design and key estimates reported.
2. Background/Rationale: clinical and epidemiologic motivation provided.
3. Objectives: primary and secondary objectives stated.
4. Study design: retrospective cohort design described.
5. Setting: single regional health system analyzed at division level; broader monitoring feed spans 10 divisions and the clustered sensitivity fit uses 9 intervention divisions.
6. Participants: monitor-unit eligibility rules, outcome-defined intervention/control membership, the 43-event study-window adjudicated cohort, the 40-event inferential base, and the distinct 91/32/30-31 descriptive cohorts are documented.
7. Variables: exposures, outcomes, covariates, and sensitivity definitions described.
8. Data sources/measurement: AI monitoring, virtual observer workflow context, linkage pipeline, outcome-defined cohort provenance, and the adjudicated STROBE pathway are specified.
9. Bias: label quality, calibration risk, residual confounding, and misclassification risks discussed; sensitivity analyses added.
10. Study size: source monitors, eligible monitors, inferential events, broader monitoring-feed events, broader observation events, and benchmark truth/scored-sequence counts are reported.
11. Quantitative variables: exposure fractions and rate-model construction described.
12. Statistical methods: Poisson GLM with offset, HC3 sensitivity, clustered exploratory fit, and dispersion diagnostics listed.
13. Participant flow: documented in `docs/strobe_flow_diagram.md`, including the manuscript-facing `43 -> 40` adjudication/linkage funnel and the separate broader descriptive cohorts.
14. Descriptive data: cohort and exposure summaries provided.
15. Outcome data: event counts and rates by position provided.
16. Main results: adjusted RR with confidence interval and p-value reported.
17. Other analyses: subgroup, threshold, and misclassification sensitivity analyses reported.
18. Key results: aligned with objectives.
19. Limitations: sparse events, label quality, and residual confounding discussed.
20. Interpretation: cautious, evidence-graded interpretation.
21. Generalizability: single-system scope explicitly acknowledged.
22. Funding/COI: disclosed in dedicated manuscript sections.
